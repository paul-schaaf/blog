I'm a software engineer with a BSc in CS from [TUM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich).
Before uni, I worked on carsharing services in Germany.
At uni, I did computational biology in the [Gagneur lab](https://www.cs.cit.tum.de/cmm/), was on TUM's synbio [iGEM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Genetically_Engineered_Machine) team, and worked on the core team of [Anchor](https://crates.io/crates/anchor-lang), an open source Rust framework.

While finishing uni, severe RSI kept me from typing much, so for a while I coded by voice with [Talon](https://talonvoice.com/) and [Cursorless](https://youtu.be/NcUJnmBqHTY). I also created [a VS Code extension](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=PaulSchaaf.talon-filetree) to navigate VS Code by voice.

After uni, I worked at [Tools for Humanity](https://www.toolsforhumanity.com/) on [worldcoin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_(blockchain)), first in Munich and then in San Francisco. I was an IC on transaction optimization and AI, and tech lead of the growth team.

I'm currently exploring opportunities in AI, science, and automation.
